What kind of game is Silver Pines?

Silver Pines is a survival horror metroidvania set in small-town America. You play Red Walker, a private investigator looking into the disappearance of musician Eddie Velvet in the half-abandoned town of Silver Pines. Expect exploration-heavy progression, nightmare encounters, puzzle solving and deliberate, scarcity-driven resource management — the official description stresses that supplies are limited and every decision counts.

The tone is deliberately unsettling small-town Americana; press coverage around the announcement compared its mood to Lynch-style mystery rather than action-first horror. If you enjoy slow-burn dread, item scarcity and a map that opens up as you regain tools, it sits squarely in that tradition.
