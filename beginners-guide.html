Before you buy (or right after)

  • Play the free demo. It has been on Steam since June 1, 2026 and is the most reliable way to judge whether Silver Pines' pacing fits you. Demo details.
  • Check your PC. Minimum is a 64-bit Windows 10 machine with 8 GB RAM and a GTX 660 Ti-class GPU. Full requirements.
  • Grab the launch discount. Steam lists $22.49 with a 10% launch discount — the cheapest official price on day one.

Confirmed mechanics to plan around

  • Scarce resources are the core loop. The official description says supplies are limited and must be carefully managed. Practically: don't burn rare items on encounters you can avoid, and treat shops/storage as strategic decisions, not chores.
  • It's a metroidvania — expect doors you can't open yet. Exploration-heavy design means you will return to old areas with new tools. Leave yourself breadcrumbs (in-game map markers or notes) instead of bashing locked paths.
  • Puzzles are part of progression. The official materials promise puzzle solving alongside combat. If a room fights you, the answer is usually observation, not grinding.
  • Nightmare encounters exist. Combat with "nightmarish terrors" is confirmed — but as a survival horror title, fleeing and conserving is a legitimate strategy, especially early.

Setup tips (all verified on the Steam store page)

  • Controller is fully supported, including DualShock/DualSense features — wired or Bluetooth both work on Windows.
  • Enable Steam Cloud in the game's properties if you play on more than one PC; it's a confirmed feature.
  • Achievements exist — if you chase them, check the in-game achievement list early so you don't miss missable ones.
  • Single-player only. No co-op or multiplayer — plan sessions accordingly.
